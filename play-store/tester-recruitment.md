# Tester Recruitment

Personal Play Console accounts created after Nov 2023 must run **14+ days of Closed testing with at least 20 testers** before promoting to Production.

The testers don't need to actively use the app. They just need to opt in to the testing track and have the app installed throughout the 14-day window.

## Step 1 — Set up the Closed Testing track in Play Console

(After uploading your first AAB to **Internal testing** and verifying it works:)

1. Play Console → your app → Testing → **Closed testing**
2. Click **Create new track** (or pick the auto-created "Alpha" track)
3. Name: "Forest Shuffle 1.0 closed beta"
4. **Testers** section:
   - Create a new email list (e.g., "Forest Shuffle Beta")
   - Add tester emails here, OR
   - Choose **Open by invitation** to get a shareable opt-in URL
5. **Releases** section → promote the AAB you previously uploaded to internal testing → release notes (see below)
6. **Save** + start rollout

You'll get an opt-in link like `https://play.google.com/apps/testing/app.forestshuffle.companion` — share this with recruits.

## Step 2 — Where to recruit

Easiest sources, ranked by likely yield:

1. **Personal network**: WhatsApp group / friends / family with Android phones (~5–10 people)
2. **Forest Shuffle BGG forum** — https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/391163/forest-shuffle, post in the General forum
3. **r/boardgames on Reddit** — post in their weekly self-promo thread
4. **Forest Shuffle Discord/Facebook groups** — search "Forest Shuffle" community on Discord; there are 1–2 active servers
5. **Kosmos newsletter list** (if you have it) — unlikely but worth checking

## Timeline expectation

- Day 0: upload AAB to Closed Testing, recruit testers
- Days 1–3: testers opt in and install
- Day 14+: Play Console reports the track as eligible
- Submit promotion to Production
- 1–3 days: Google reviews
- App live

Realistic minimum: **17–18 days** from first AAB upload to public Play Store listing.
