# Play Store Submission Materials

Drafted listing content, copy-paste-ready for the Google Play Console.

- **`listing.md`** — App title, short/full description, category, contact details, privacy URL.
- **`data-safety.md`** — Answers to the Data Safety form.
- **`content-rating.md`** — Expected answers to the content-rating questionnaire (auto-generates a PEGI/ESRB rating).
- **`screenshots.md`** — Guide for what to capture and the size requirements.
- **`feature-graphic.md`** — Specs and design directions for the 1024×500 banner.
- **`tester-recruitment.md`** — Ready-to-post messages for recruiting the 20+ closed-test users.

After your Play Console ID verification completes, work through each file in order and paste the content into the matching Play Console section.
