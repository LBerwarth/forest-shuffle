## Full description (max 4000 chars)

```
Forest Shuffle Scorer is a free, ad-free score calculator and game tracker for the Forest Shuffle board game by Kosmos, including the Dartmoor expansion.

After every game, run the in-app wizard step by step — Trees, Top, Bottom, Lateral, Cave, Moor — and the app does all the scoring math for you. Every card's points, set bonuses, multipliers, and special rules are handled automatically.

FEATURES

• Full support for both editions: Classic Forest Shuffle and Forest Shuffle: Dartmoor
• Step-by-step scoring wizard with built-in card icons, search, and per-category filter chips (filter by Bat, Bird, Plant, etc.)
• Live multi-device scoring sessions: one host starts a game, every player joins on their own phone, all scores merge at the end
• Score breakdowns per card, per category, per player — including the Violet Carpenter Bee's +1-of-host-tree effect handled automatically
• Game history with full results and per-game insights
• Stats dashboards: average scores, win streaks, top-scoring cards, tag-based analytics, "synergy" view showing which tags enable the most points across your games
• Global Hall of Fame: anonymous worldwide leaderboard of highest scores and biggest single-card plays, filterable by player count
• Available in English, German, French, Spanish

PRIVACY

This app does not show ads. It does not track you for marketing. It does not collect your name, email, phone, or location. It stores game scores and the player nicknames you choose, so you can sync your history across devices.

DISCLAIMER

This is an unofficial fan-made companion. Forest Shuffle is © Kosmos / Lookout. This app does not redistribute card text, card art, or game rules; you still need the physical game to play.

Questions, bugs, suggestions: lena.berw@gmail.com
```
