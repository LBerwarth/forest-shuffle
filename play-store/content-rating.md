PEGI: **3** (suitable for all ages)

Apple-equivalent: **4+**

Why not "Everyone" outright? The UGC + interaction flags push it up a notch in some jurisdictions, but for a board-game companion this is normal.

## If a question is ambiguous

When in doubt, answer **No**. Play Store auto-rates lenient apps as PEGI 3 / Everyone, and that's what you want. The only "Yes" that genuinely applies is user-interaction in live sessions, and Play handles that as a soft note, not a content escalation.
