# Screenshots Guide

Play Console requires **2 to 8 phone screenshots**. Ideal: 4–6 well-chosen views.

## Technical requirements

- **Format:** PNG or JPEG
- **Aspect ratio:** Between 16:9 and 9:16
- **Min short side:** 320 px
- **Max long side:** 3840 px
- **Recommended size:** 1080 × 2400 (modern Android phone aspect, portrait)
- **Max file size:** 8 MB each

## How to capture them

1. On your Android phone, open Chrome → go to https://forest-shuffle-app.vercel.app
2. Chrome menu → **Add to Home screen** → confirm
3. Launch the installed PWA from your home screen
4. For each of the views below: navigate to it, then take a screenshot (volume-down + power button on most Android phones, or the screenshot gesture)
5. Move the PNGs to your computer (USB, Drive, AirDrop equivalent)

## Recommended 6 screenshots

| # | View | What to capture | Suggested caption |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Home page** | The landing screen with main actions visible | "Start a new game in seconds" |
| 2 | **New game wizard — Tree step** | Mid-game with a few tree cards counted, filter chips visible | "Filter cards by category as you score" |
| 3 | **New game wizard — Lateral with carpenter bee picker** | Show the host-tree picker for the bee | "Special rules handled automatically" |
| 4 | **Results page** | A finished game with the leaderboard | "See every card's contribution after the game" |
| 5 | **Stats tab — overview** | The leaderboard + at-a-glance + tag insights | "Track every player and every card" |
| 6 | **Stats tab — Hall of Fame** | The Hall of Fame card visible | "Compete on the global leaderboard" |

## Tablet screenshots (optional)

Skip unless you have an Android tablet. The app is responsive, but Play allows phone-only listings.

## Tips

- Take screenshots in **English** as the default. You can localise later if you add other languages.
- Make sure no real personal player names show (use "Anna", "Bob", "Carl" or similar generic names).
- Pick a game with **interesting numbers** (varied scores, multiple players, several different cards) — boring screenshots look like an unfinished app.
- The status bar (battery, time, signal) is visible in screenshots and that's fine — many real Play Store listings show this.

## Where to upload them

Play Console → Main store listing → **Phone screenshots** → drag-and-drop or click upload.
