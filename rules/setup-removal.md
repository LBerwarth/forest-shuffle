Other setup notes the app shows:

- Winter cards: split the rest into 3 piles, shuffle 2 winter cards into one, 3rd on top of that pile, pile goes to the bottom of the deck (all editions).
- Solo: only 1 winter card, shuffled into the bottom 10 cards; 6 cards in hand plus 5 face up in the clearing (Exploration rules p. 2).
- Exploration special caves replace the base caves, chosen in reverse turn order (Exploration rules p. 7).
- Dartmoor caves: one per player in the clearing, chosen in reverse turn order, effect resolved immediately (Dartmoor rules p. 3).
- Exmoor caves replace the Dartmoor caves (Exmoor rules p. 1).

The base rulebook PDF is not in this folder: lookout-spiele.de serves its PDFs only to browsers.
The Alpine, Exploration, Dartmoor and Automa PDFs here were saved through a browser fetch on 2026-09-30.
